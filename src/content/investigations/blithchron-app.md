## The constraint

Event software is shaped by changing schedules, many stakeholders, and an immovable deadline. As lead frontend developer, I worked on translating that moving system into a coherent mobile experience.

## What it tested

The work required more than implementing screens. It tested communication, interface consistency, prioritization, and the ability to keep a shared system understandable while it changed.

## The lesson

Complex systems are often social before they are technical. A strong implementation depends on making decisions legible to the people building it together.
