## The starting point

Short-form creators often need music that already fits the duration and tone of a small piece of content. MusicLib began as an attempt to make that constraint the center of the product rather than an afterthought.

## What building revealed

Publishing transformed a straightforward app idea into a systems problem: content organization, interface decisions, mobile constraints, distribution, and maintenance all had to agree. The project became useful evidence that an elegant feature is only one part of a dependable product.

## What remains useful

The lasting lesson is not a particular mobile framework. It is the discipline of carrying an idea through implementation, release, and real-world constraints.
